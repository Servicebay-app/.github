## What it is

WrenchEngine keeps your technicians prepared, your jobs moving and your billing connected, from the first call to the final invoice. It connects dispatch, service orders, parts, scheduling and billing, so the whole team carries the same job record through the service day.

## One connected service journey

| | Step | What happens |
|:-:|---|---|
| 01 | **Request** | The fleet reports the problem and requests service. Dispatch captures what the customer needs. |
| 02 | **Prepare** | Dispatch prepares the assignment, so the technician starts with the job already in context. |
| 03 | **Repair** | The technician confirms the fault, the customer approves the repair, and the approved work is completed and documented. |
| 04 | **Invoice** | The invoice is prepared from the work recorded. A person reviews and approves it before sending. |
| 05 | **Maintain** | Calendar, mileage and engine-hour triggers schedule the next service, mobile or shop. |
| 06 | **Manage** | Authorized leaders see the priorities that need follow-up, with optional Cockpit. |
